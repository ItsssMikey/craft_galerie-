Erling Valtyrson · Kjellerbilde · Mezzotint · 21,5 × 24,5 cm

MEZZOTINT; refleksjoner

Erling Valtyrson
30 May – 5 July 2026

The opening exhibition at CRAFT presents Erling Valtyrson's intricate mezzotints — a printmaking technique known for its deep tonal range and meticulous, hand-burnished surfaces. The works invite quiet, sustained looking; still lifes and interiors composed from objects that hover between the everyday and the symbolic.
